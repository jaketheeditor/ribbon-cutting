03 — Cultural Interstitials
Catawba Heroes
Photo Montage

Alongside the speaker backdrop, we've designed a dedicated video montage celebrating the heroes of Catawba Nation — featuring historical portraits, cultural imagery, and the stories behind the people who shaped the community. This is not a static slide show; each frame is a designed, animated tribute built in the same visual language as the event backdrop.

Loop Duration
The montage is designed to run on a 5+ minute continuous loop — long enough to feel rich and unhurried, short enough to reward repeat viewing throughout the event.
When It Plays
The montage fills the LED wall between speakers — during transitions, networking breaks, and arrival. It can also bookend the event, running before the programme begins and after the final speaker.
Intent
Each slide is a designed tribute to a Catawba hero — not a static photograph but an animated, branded moment. Together they honour the culture and people of the Catawba Nation throughout the entire event.
04 — Motion Design
Animation System
1
Background Live
0s — continuous
Evolving gradient shifts slowly between warm brown and cool navy. Seven wide diagonal parallelogram bars drift at varied speeds creating parallax depth. Screen blend mode brightens intersections.
2
Label Drop
0.6s
"Speaker" label descends from above with a spring overshoot — arrives with gentle bounce before settling. Establishes the zone before the boxes arrive.
3
Box Construction
0.9s → 1.2s
Name box (Box A) swings in from the right with inertia — spring cubic-bezier, slight scaleX overshoot. Title box (Box B) follows 300ms later at a different spring tension, creating staggered weight.
4
Text Reveal
2.4s — held
Once both boxes are fully settled, the name and title fade in together. A thin gold rule draws left-to-right inside the name box. Text holds for the remainder of the 30-second loop.
Background Motion Layers
Gradient Base
Three sinusoidal oscillators drive RGB channel values independently — left zone warm, right zone cool — slowly breathing between states. Period ~5–7 minutes per full cycle.
Diagonal Bars
Seven wide parallelogram planes drift left at deliberately different speeds (0.000015–0.000085 px/ms). Screen blend creates luminous intersections. Each bar 45–70% of screen width.
Arc & Line System
Eight large circular arcs sweep slowly at screen edges. Eleven diagonal drift lines cross at 22°. Fine horizontal grid lines travel up or down at opposing speeds, adding woven texture depth.

06 — Further Concepts
Four Alternative
Directions

Each concept uses the same speaker name reveal system and brand palette — only the background treatment changes. Two draw directly on Catawba cultural iconography: the pottery tradition and the woven textile heritage.

Concept A · Catawba Iconography
Pottery Marks
The Catawba Nation carries one of the Southeast's last unbroken pottery traditions. This backdrop makes that heritage visible — a ghosted field of diamond and chevron forms drawn from traditional Catawba vessel patterns drifts slowly across a deep terracotta-to-ink gradient. The geometry is spare and deliberate, never decorative for its own sake.
Terracotta · Amber · Gold
Concept B · Elemental
The River
Catawba means "people of the river." This concept translates that identity into motion — slow-flowing horizontal wave bands in deep navy and midnight blue suggest the Catawba River at dusk. A gold shimmer line runs at the horizon; fine sparkle particles drift like light on water. Serene and ceremonial.
Deep Navy · Steel · Gold
Concept C · Atmospheric
Ember Field
A near-black field of rising gold and amber particles — like embers lifting from a fire, or stars above open land. Warm bloom zones pulse slowly beneath the surface. The most cinematic and intimate of the four directions. Works especially well for evening events.
Ink · Ember · Gold
Concept D · Catawba Iconography
Woven Nation
Catawba basketweave and textile traditions are among the community's most enduring art forms. Two interlocking diagonal thread grids — drifting at different speeds — create a living weave across a deep warm-brown ground. Gold thread lines catch light at the intersections. Earthy, structured, and unmistakably Catawba.
Deep Brown · Amber · Gold Thread
